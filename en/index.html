Individual therapy

  • I work with clients aged 18 and over. Starting therapy must be a person's own voluntary decision.
  • We meet in a safe, calm space for 50 minutes once a week and work with the topics that are important and painful for you:
  • relationships with other people (romantic, work, parents, children, etc.)
  • relationship with yourself (self-esteem, ideal and real self, identity crises, etc.)
  • existential crises (Who am I? Why am I here? Why? What should I do?)
  • grief, saying goodbye to what has been lost, and searching for something new
  • anxiety, body processes, sexuality
  • life in emigration and other topics
